01About

I lead Flutter development at Diode Info Solutions, building cross-platform apps for clients across Bahrain, Morocco and beyond. My speciality is the multi-app ecosystem — interconnected suites of three or four apps that together run a whole business.

Beyond apps, I publish Flutter packages — hardware printer drivers, payment gateway SDKs, ML-powered photo analysis — tools other developers use in their own projects.

  • A

    Enterprise scale

    Led mobile development for 7 multi-app ecosystems, each with 2–4 interconnected applications.

  • B

    Multi-country

    Apps serving users in Bahrain, Morocco and other markets, with full localisation support.

  • C

    Hardware & IoT

    BLE smartwatches, thermal printers, USB cameras and payment terminals integrated into Flutter.

  • D

    Open source

    Six published packages: printer drivers, payment SDKs and on-device ML.

v1.2.2

Flutter plugin for Nyx printers (NB55, T2 Pro, T2 Plus) — print text, barcodes, QR codes and images via Android AIDL service binding.

  • PNG printing
  • Android 14+

afs_gateway

v1.0.0

AFS Benefit Payment Gateway SDK — AES-256-CBC encryption, automated 3DS2 authentication, JS bridge result interception and deep-link support.

  • AES-256-CBC
  • 3DS2
  • JS bridge
  • Sandbox cards

photo_analyzer

v0.2.4

ML-powered photo analysis — face detection, gender estimation and NSFW classification with TensorFlow Lite on-device inference.

  • NSFW classification

06Journey

How I got here.

  1. 2021

    First Flutter projects

    Began professional Flutter development. Early work included e-commerce apps, games (Ludo, Snake & Ladder) and HRMS systems — the fundamentals of cross-platform mobile.

  2. 2022

    Multi-app ecosystems

    Safar Cab (rider + driver), Exodus (3-app trucking platform) and Hospivio (3-app hospital suite). Learned to architect interconnected systems that share logic and APIs.

  3. 2023

    Payment SDKs & restaurant tech

    Went deep on restaurant POS with Chicket and Lime POS. Built the AFS Benefit gateway SDK from scratch — AES-256, 3DS2 and JS bridge interception. Wafaa+ hit its stride.

  4. 2024

    IoT & hardware

    Extended Flutter into hardware: the Neurya smartwatch SDK over BLE, the Nyx thermal-printer plugin and a UVC camera plugin. Published packages to the Flutter community.

  5. 2025
    — now

    Full-stack Flutter

    Leading Atherminds (4-app smart-TV ad platform), Norda Kitchen (cloud kitchen with health API) and Lime POS Management (Windows desktop). ML-powered packages with TFLite. 30+ apps shipped and counting.
