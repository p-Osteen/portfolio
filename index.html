About

Turning ideas into
production-ready apps

I'm a Senior Flutter Developer at Diode Info Solutions, where I lead the development of cross-platform mobile applications for clients across Bahrain, Morocco, and beyond.

Over 5 years, I've shipped 30+ production applications spanning restaurant POS systems, ride-hailing platforms, hospital management suites, loyalty ecosystems, digital signage networks, and IoT wearable integrations. I specialize in building multi-app ecosystems — interconnected suites of 3–4 apps that work together to power complete business operations.

Beyond apps, I create and publish Flutter packages — from hardware printer drivers to payment gateway SDKs — contributing tools that other developers use in their own projects.

Journey

How I got here

2021 – Getting Started
First Flutter Projects
Began professional Flutter development. Built early projects including e-commerce apps, games (Ludo, Snake & Ladder), and HRMS systems. Learned the fundamentals of cross-platform mobile development.
2022 – Building Scale
Multi-App Ecosystems
Graduated to building multi-app ecosystems — Safar Cab (rider + driver), Exodus (3-app trucking platform), and Hospivio (3-app hospital suite). Learned to architect interconnected systems that share logic and APIs.
2023 – Specialization
Payment SDKs & Restaurant Tech
Deep-dived into the restaurant/POS space with Chicket and Lime POS. Built the AFS Benefit Payment Gateway SDK from scratch — handling AES-256 encryption, 3DS2, and JS bridge interception. Wafaa+ loyalty platform hit its stride.
2024 – IoT & Hardware
Beyond the Screen
Extended Flutter into hardware — built Neurya SDK for smartwatches over BLE, Nyx Printer plugin for thermal receipt printers, and UVC Camera plugin for USB cameras. Published packages to the Flutter community.
2025 – Present
Full-Stack Flutter Mastery
Leading development on Atherminds (4-app smart TV ad platform), Norda Kitchen (cloud kitchen with health API), and Lime POS Management (Windows desktop). Building ML-powered packages with TFLite. 30+ apps shipped and counting.
