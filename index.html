About

Turning ideas into production-ready apps

I lead Flutter development at Diode Info Solutions, building cross-platform apps for clients across Bahrain, Morocco and beyond. My speciality is the multi-app ecosystem — suites of three or four interconnected apps that together run a whole business.

I also publish Flutter packages on pub.dev — a Nyx printer driver and an on-device photo analysis toolkit — that other developers use in their own projects.

  • Enterprise scale

    Led mobile development for 7 multi-app ecosystems, each with 2–4 interconnected applications.

  • Multi-country

    Apps serving users in Bahrain, Morocco and other markets, with full localisation.

  • Hardware & IoT

    BLE smartwatches, thermal printers, USB cameras and payment terminals integrated into Flutter.

  • Open source

    Two packages on pub.dev: a thermal-printer driver and on-device photo analysis.
